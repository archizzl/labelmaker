## 1. How permissions work (summary)
- **Roles:** owner/admin can do everything; member = what their member type grants per section (none/view/edit), optionally "own bands only" (`src/lib/permissions.ts`). No member type means only `/me`.
- **`requireAccess(area, level)`** (`src/server/context.ts:455`) is the gate for pages and actions; failing it redirects to `/me` with no message.
- **Label-made band account ("lens"):** works in the label's books, limited to its band, with fixed rights `LENS_ACCESS` (context.ts:221): edit orders, inventory, catalog, roster; view sales, payouts, funds, receipts. For dashboard/sales/payouts/receipts/rules it switches to its own books. There, its shared login is admin and a member's own login can only view.
- **View as:** a label admin sees the app as a band or person, read-only; enforced by refusing server actions (context.ts:208), and in Abel by refusing write tools (abel/tool.ts:53).
- **Site admin:** email in `SITE_ADMIN_EMAILS` (site-admin.ts).

## 2. Permission problems

### Fix now
1. ✔ **A band's login can overwrite any of the label's split rules.** `saveSplitRule` (actions.ts:532-575) checks only that `ruleId` belongs to the label, not that it is the rule for the band's own release, then rewrites it. Passing the label default's id turns the label-wide split into a split for one album. The fix: in the lens branch, require the existing rule to be a release/track rule on the band's own item, and ignore `bandId`. The same applies to `deleteSplitRule`.
2. ✔ **"View as" shows the person's Abel chats.** api/abel/route.ts:37 takes the viewed person's user id (context.ts:398), so GET lists and opens their chats, and POST would save chats as them. Fix: Abel off (or chats empty and nothing saved) while viewing as.
3. **Band-limited sales editors aren't limited on import and matching.** `assignRouting`, `assignArtistToBand`, `deleteRoutingOverride`, `rerouteAction`, `deleteImport`, `commitImport`, `previewImport` (actions.ts ~800-870) check `sales:edit` only. Fix: add the band-scope check, or require unlimited sales edit for these label-wide actions.
4. **Band accounts get 404 on their own receipt files.** receipts/file/[id]/route.ts:15 uses the lens context, but own-books receipts are in the band's orgId (`canViewReceipt`, expenses.ts:197). Fix: check against own books too. Merch photos likely have the same problem (merch/photo/[id]/route.ts:18).
5. ✔ **Inventory comments and `bandMay` contradict the code.** `LENS_ACCESS` already grants inventory edit, so `bandMay` (only used for inventory) does nothing, and the inventory page's "counts only" split never applies. Fix: decide (see Your call #4), then either drop inventory edit from `LENS_ACCESS` or remove `bandMay` and the `canCount` split, and correct the comments (context.ts:146, inventory-actions.ts:177, inventory/page.tsx:32).
6. **Edit buttons shown to view-only users.** Payout view (Mark paid, Undo finalize, Release unsold: payout-view.tsx:124,206,434,489), Directory and band pages (merge, delete, add member, save) and the dashboard's "Import sales"/"Pay out". Clicking bounces to `/me`. Fix: hide them when `can(…, "edit")` is false.
7. `setLabelAccountHolder` (actions.ts:1146) checks funds:edit, but its form is on admin-only Settings: require admin.
8. `syncWithBandcamp`'s lens branch (actions.ts:776) has no access check: require the band's shared login.
9. ✔ Wording: "Only admins can delete this." (expense-actions.ts:313) when the check is receipts edit.
10. Abel's page list uses `isAdmin` for Settings (read-tools.ts:524), but the nav shows Settings to a band's shared login: match the nav.

### Your call (product decisions)
1. ✔ **Shipping costs skip approval.** Postage/packaging entered on "Mark shipped" become *approved* receipts, paid by any person the shipper picks (shipment-costs.ts:36-82). A band login or band-limited manager can create money owed to themselves. Keep it, or make them pending receipts?
2. **Band accounts can change their band's matching.** `saveBand` lets a band's login change its aliases/URL patterns (actions.ts:163-197), which re-matches the label's sales, despite the comment "Not the band itself". Allow it, or keep those fields label-only?
3. **Roster edit includes payees' PayPal/Venmo.** Band logins have it, so they can redirect where a bandmate's payout goes. OK, or should a person's payment details be theirs (and admins') only?
4. **What a band account may do in inventory:** everything (current behaviour) or counts only (what the comments say)?
5. **Site admin by email with open sign-up and no email verification.** Safe only while every admin address already has an account. Fine as is, or tie site admin to user ids?
6. **Any admin, not only the owner, can delete the whole group** (`deleteAccount`, account-actions.ts:576). Owner only?
7. **Dashboard ignores band limits:** a band-limited member with dashboard access sees every band's totals. Scope it, or treat dashboard as label-wide by design?
8. **Plain members can add hand-entered sales that count in payouts** (manual-sales.ts:16-38). Intended?

## 3. Workflow problems

### Fix now
1. ✔ **"Mark all as paid" marks more than it says.** `markAllPaid` (actions.ts:1131) marks every pending line, including 0/negative lines and lines bands pay themselves. The confirm counts only lines above 0 (payout-view.tsx:51,492), and says "1 unpaid payouts". Fix: same filter on both sides, skip lines bands pay, use the `plural` helper.
2. ✔ **Checklist points to a hidden card.** "Decide what the label keeps" links to `/rules#label-cut`, but that card is commented out (rules/page.tsx:58-113), so a label that keeps nothing can't tick off the step. Fix: bring the card back, or drop the step (Your call #1 below).
3. **Money a band sent can be recorded twice.** The band (`recordPaymentToLabel`) and the label (`saveBandRemittance`) both write `band_remittances`, so the same transfer is counted twice, and the band side can't delete. Fix: show each side the other's entries and give the band a delete.
4. **A band can't undo "paid" on a member** (band-sends.tsx:24 lists only pending lines). Add Undo, which the action already supports.
5. **Rejected receipts can't be reopened.** Add "Undo reject" (back to pending).
6. **Removed inventory items can't come back,** and "Add from the catalog" skips them too. Add a restore, or let add-from-catalog bring archived ones back.
7. **Errors from plain forms reach the generic error page** (deleteBand "has payouts", reviewExpense's recoup check, saveBand/savePerson). On the live site the message is hidden. Fix: switch those forms to `ActionForm` and return `{ error }`.
8. **Outside artist "change" deletes the contact straight away** (outside-artists.tsx:96). Rename it to Remove with a confirm, or make it open an edit form.
9. **Join link without an account loses the link** (join/[id]/page.tsx:131 → `/signup` with no `next`). Pass `next`.
10. **Login page advice:** "Ask whoever runs your label or band for a reset link", but only site admins can make one. Fix the text: whoever runs labelmaker makes it.
11. **Merging duplicates:** "keep this one" (directory:218) merges with no confirm; the other merge warns. Add the same confirm.

### Your call
1. **Hidden cards still in use:** "What the label keeps" and "How sales are matched" (Forget / Re-match all) are commented out, but the checklist and import's "Assign & remember" depend on them, so a wrong remembered match can't be undone. Bring them back somewhere?
2. **No way to add a person by hand.** Since "invite with a link", nobody without a login (a producer, a member with no email) can be given a split. The checklist still says "Add members to each band", and Abel still adds people by hand.
3. **Inviting needs a member type first.** There's no plain "invite" on Who can sign in; the only links are per member type. Add a plain invite (admin, or member with no type)?
4. **Who can sign in / Member types:** on Settings for labels, but on the Directory for bands. Same place for both?
5. **Per-band invite links never expire and can't be reset** (reset button commented out, band-invite-links.tsx:30). The label's link for bands can be reset.
6. **"Undo finalize" deletes a fully paid payout's records,** including lines bands marked paid. Block it once anything is paid?
7. **No way to make a second group** from inside the app (only by typing `/welcome`).
8. **Fans:** hidden from the nav but reachable, and still a grantable permission. Remove the permission while it's hidden?
9. **Leftovers to delete:** `ApplicationForm`/`applyForAccount`, `resetInviteLink`, the email invite page (only Abel reaches it), Abel's `adjust_inventory` reasons (the counter now logs only +/− and "set").

## 4. Design problems

### Fix now (rule breaks; mechanical)
1. **"e.g." in the interface:** inventory/page.tsx:303,331,358, application-form.tsx:85-86, band-fields.tsx:22, catalog/[id]:356, deduction-form.tsx:182.
2. **Plurals that don't change for 1:** sales/import:170,270,311; payout-view.tsx:115,492,549; sales/page.tsx:295. Use `plural()`.
3. **Empty states with advice:** sales:243-249, catalog:53, bands/[id]:359,470,583, periods:133, catalog/[id]:290, me:51, fans:218.
4. **Explanatory intros and hints:** bands/[id]:303,457,702; settings:314; band-fields.tsx:14; directory:220,520,543; deduction-form:193; sales/import:175-186,220; orders:57,190; receipts/page:152; signup:43; dashboard hints page.tsx:100,114; import-form:134; split-editor:184; payout-view:486 and the "Finalizing saves…" note (payout-view:515).
5. **Group/account wording:** "Already have a login?" (signup:33), "create a login" (invite:92), "the account that runs your band" (join:116,62), "label or band" (login:18, welcome:24, signup:44). Bank account wording: "Label bank account", "label account" badge, "Left in the label's account" (funds:142, label-funds:166, me:23, payout-view:446) should say "bank account" so it isn't confused with a person's account.
6. **Jargon:** "unrouted" vs "unmatched" in the same delete-band text (bands/[id]:702-703), "txn", "Superseded", "carve-outs"; "Band funds and costs" card whose button says "+ Add deduction".
7. **Dark mode:** set `color-scheme: light dark` so date pickers, selects and scrollbars follow the theme.

### Your call (consistency; bigger)
1. **Seven tab bars with different styles** (nav, band, funds, sales, admin, sales section, band filter): one `Tabs` component?
2. **Editing works four ways** (click a row to expand, "Edit" panel, standalone toggle, "Change"), and a person has two different edit forms (band People tab vs Directory) with different fields, save buttons and remove wording. Pick one pattern and one person form?
3. **Feedback:** 99 plain forms give no confirmation; success shows 4 ways; busy labels vary ("Working…", "…", "Saving…"). Move the plain forms to `ActionForm`?
4. **Delete buttons:** red in some places, grey in others; "Forget" (rules) has no confirm; deleting a band (releases, splits, deductions) is a single yes/no while deleting a group needs its name typed.
5. **Stat tiles in two styles** (~12 hand-built), hand-written empty states and link-buttons, no shared date format (ISO, "Month D, YYYY", ranges, with and without UTC), "–" vs "—".
6. **Contrast:** white on the teal accent is ~3:1 (buttons, nav count pills).
